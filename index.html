Our mission

Less than 4% of federal cancer research funding goes to childhood cancer. For kids with cancer, that gap can be the difference between a clinical trial existing and not existing.

Rebels Fight Cancer is a student-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our chapters run events, fundraisers, and awareness drives, and we send the proceeds to the National Pediatric Cancer Foundation, which funds research on experimental treatments for kids. Our first chapter is at Great Neck South High School, and new chapters are opening as students bring the club to their own schools.

About the board

Co-Founder, Co-President & Operations Lead

Aayan Alwani

Aayan is a junior at Great Neck South High School. He co-founded Rebels Fight Cancer because of a long-running interest in cancer research and biology, and wanted to put that interest toward something that helps kids directly. He works on growing the organization: finding local businesses to host our fundraisers and getting students at other schools involved with pediatric cancer.

Co-Founder, Co-President & Finance Lead

Oliver Won

Oliver is a senior at Great Neck South High School. He co-founded Rebels Fight Cancer to combine his experience in leadership and service with a cause that could mobilize his school community. He coordinates fundraisers, card drives, and awareness events that support pediatric cancer research. Outside the organization, Oliver serves as president of Great Neck South DECA, teaches piano, and volunteers with Tzu Chi USA as a team leader and English tutor.

Co-Founder, Co-President & Marketing Lead

Olivia Lee

Olivia is a passionate rising senior at Great Neck South High School with experience when it comes to business, content writing, and community engagement. She first got involved with Rebels Fight Cancer because she wanted to help others around her, but eventually that love grew into a passion for empowering others to make a difference in their communities. Through Rebels Fight Cancer, Olivia has discovered how powerful it can be to bring people together around a cause that matters, and she hopes to continue using her creativity, leadership, and voice to create meaningful change.
